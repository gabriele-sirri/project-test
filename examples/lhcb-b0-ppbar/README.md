# LHCb: $B^0 \to p\bar p$

**Field:** flavour physics · **Used in:** Lab 1 (Exercises B and C), Lab 2 (Exercises A and C) · Part of the labs: not available as a project case.

**Question.** Is there a signal in the proton–antiproton invariant mass over a combinatorial background, and how many events?

**Inspired by.** LHCb, “First observation of the rare purely baryonic decay $B^0\to p\bar p$”. Simplified problem built for teaching.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `data/rarest_b0_decay.dat` | 166 candidate masses $m(p\bar p)$ in MeV (5084–5541), one per line |
| `data/b0_invariant_mass.root` | TH1F `massaB0`: B-meson mass spectrum, 100 bins in 5–6 GeV, 760 entries |

**What the course does.** Lab 1: extended fit signal + background, Breit–Wigner and resolution. Lab 2: MIGRAD/HESSE/MINOS, profile, toys and pulls.
