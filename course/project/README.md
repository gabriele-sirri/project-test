# Team project

One analysis, built across the four labs, in **teams of 2–3**.
Pick a case below, register in [`teams.md`](teams.md), and present in **Lab 4**.

## The cases

| # | Case | Field | Core question | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CUORE-0 | nuclear | Is there a peak at the $^{130}$Te $Q$-value? If not, what limit? | `examples/cuore0` |
| 2 | IceCube | neutrino | Oscillation parameters from atmospheric $\nu_\mu$ disappearance | `examples/icecube-oscillations` |
| 3 | MINOS | neutrino | $\Delta m^2$, $\sin^2 2\theta$ from an energy spectrum | `examples/minos-oscillations` |
| 4 | MAGIC | astroparticle | $\gamma$/hadron separation and significance | `examples/magic-gamma-hadron` |
| 5 | ENUBET test beam | instrumentation | Fraction of double hits and the resolution | `examples/enubet-test-beam` |
| 6 | **Your own case** | any | see below | — |

Each folder has a README with the question, the data, and a milestone plan for the labs.
The cases seen in class (LHCb, top-quark on/off, OPERA, CMS $H\to4\ell$) are **not** available as projects.

**Assignment.** At most **two teams per case**, by registration order. A second team on the same case should take a different approach (another model, another systematic).

**Your own case.** Welcome if you have shareable data and a clear question. Send a **one-page proposal by Lab 2** (question, data, model you have in mind, the systematic you expect). The instructor says yes or no.

## Milestones

| By | Everyone has |
|---|---|
| **Lab 2** | team registered, case chosen, data read, a first fit |
| **Lab 3** | errors studied (HESSE vs MINOS), **one systematic** as a nuisance parameter, an interval, a limit or a p-value |
| **Lab 4** | a binned or workspace version of the model, final result, **3–5 minute showcase** |

Each lab leaves time for the project. Use the weeks between labs.

## The showcase: one figure, one number, one caveat

- **One figure:** the plot that carries your result, readable from the back of the room.
- **One number:** your main result with its uncertainty or interval, and what it means.
- **One caveat:** the most important limitation. What does your result *not* tell us?

No slides are needed. Say who did what: everyone speaks.

## What to hand in

- A notebook or macro, runnable top to bottom.
- A **one-page report**: question, model, result, caveat, and *“Who did what”*.
- Commits from every team member in your team repository.

The project is part of your work in the course and is used for the lab question at the exam.
Each member should be able to explain any part of the analysis.
